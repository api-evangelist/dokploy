We're thrilled to announce Dokploy v0.21.0, bringing exciting new features to enhance your deployment workflow! This release introduces a Gitea provider, backup and restore capabilities for Dokploy itself, and the ability to duplicate services. Gitea Provider You can now use Gitea as a provider for your Dokploy deployments!
